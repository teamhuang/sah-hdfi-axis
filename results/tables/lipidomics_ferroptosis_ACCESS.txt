RELEVANCE TO THE EXECUTION LAYER (important caveat for the reviewer):
  This human SAH-vs-control CSF study profiles intact lipid classes; it did NOT
  measure oxidised phosphatidylethanolamines (ox-PE / PE-OOH), free 4-HNE or
  MDA. The closest ferroptosis-relevant hits are AA/LA-derived SUBSTRATE or
  signalling lipids (arachidonoyl amide, PUFA-plasmalogens, PA45:4, LPC/MAG
  18:2), i.e. substrate/precursor axis, NOT the peroxidised execution species.
